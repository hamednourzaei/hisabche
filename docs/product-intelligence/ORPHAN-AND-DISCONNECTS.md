  6 duplicate definitions, 3 client utilities,
  2 with no route and no consumer.
- 72 hooks resolve to a route and have no importing component. The previous
  "74" was those 72 plus the 2 no-route/no-consumer hooks.
- The previous "2 orphan hooks" was only the last group. Both numbers described real
  rows; neither described the same set.
