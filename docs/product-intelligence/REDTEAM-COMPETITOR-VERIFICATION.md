## 1. Principles of Competitor Verification

1. Competitor features cited as benchmarks must be verified by primary documentation, active pricing matrices, or reproducible technical reality.
2. A marketing bullet point is classified as `MARKETING_CLAIM_ONLY` until supported by operational constraints.
3. Competitor features that are irrelevant, over-engineered, or counter-productive for Persian/Dari SMBs are explicitly flagged as `DO_NOT_IMITATE`.

## 3. Dangerous Traps to Avoid (What Competitors Do Wrong)

1. **The Intuit / QBO Base-Currency Trap:** Forcing all transactions to convert back to an artificial single base currency with unrealized gain/loss entries. In multi-currency bazaar economies (e.g., Kabul, Herat, Dubai, Tehran), merchants trade physical USD, AFN, IRR, and EUR cash simultaneously. Hisabche's integer multi-currency ledger without forced synthetic conversion is a massive competitive advantage.
2. **The Holoo USB Hardware Dongle Trap:** Tying licenses to physical USB keys that get lost, broken, or blocked by Windows updates. Hisabche's digital session tokens and portable encrypted SQLite snapshots provide superior user experience.
3. **The Pure-Cloud Trap (QuickBooks / Xero Architecture):** Requiring persistent broadband for all transaction saves (per Intuit QBO and Xero System Requirements). In regions with frequent connectivity cuts (e.g., Kabul or Herat), cloud-only tools halt cash register operations completely. Hisabche's local SQLite engine guarantees uninterrupted point-of-sale checkout.
