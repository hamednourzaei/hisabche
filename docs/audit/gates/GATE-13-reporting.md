## Summary

- Implemented: 27 report endpoints or families (rows above). Partial/limited: valuation (no filter), tax return (online-required). Unwired: management pack, scheduled reports. Planned/missing: the four dedicated reports in the last row.
- Money reports are in the books currency or per currency; none sums across currencies silently (consistent with CLAUDE rule).
- Exports exist as CSV buttons on the accounting tabs and the invoice list; no XLSX/PDF export for ledger reports was found outside the invoice PDF.
- Two read endpoints with money have **no capability** (reorder/dead stock, payroll summary) and the dashboard route relies on the workspace only.
