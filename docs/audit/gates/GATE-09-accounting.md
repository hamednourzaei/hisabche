# GATE-09 — Accounting audit

Status: **PASS for the audit** (every money flow listed in the protocol traced to its journal source or shown to have none). The behaviour was **not executed** against a database. No fix was made.

## 1. What the ledger accepts

`LedgerSourceType` (`backend/src/services/accounting/ledger.port.ts:24-49`): `invoice`, `payment`, `stock_adjustment`, `payroll`, `pos_session`, `depreciation`, `fx_revaluation`, `cost_repost`, `till_transfer`, `opening_capital`, `opening_stock`, `manual`, `reversal`. Account roles (`accounting.schema.ts`): bank, cash, receivable, payable, **tax**, inventory, cogs, sales, purchase, retained_earnings, current_year_earnings, salary_expense, fx_gain_loss, owner_capital, bank_fees. `postDocument` is idempotent per (source type, source id) (`already_posted`); `reverseDocument` adds a reversal entry.

There is **no source type** for: sales return, purchase return, deposit, COD, deferral, expense claim, landed cost.

## 4. Defect found in this gate

**N-37 — invoice posting carries no tax and no discount lines.** `InvoicePostingAccounts` has five roles (inventory, payable, receivable, sales, cogs); `invoiceLedgerLines` takes one `total`. For a sale with tax the whole tax goes to Sales; no tax-payable liability is created, the tax return and balance sheet cannot be reconciled to the ledger, and a return could not reverse tax. The tax engine's per-line snapshots are not consumed by the posting. Whether the business wants tax-inclusive revenue is a policy; there is no policy setting and no test of the VAT case (`rg tax backend/src/__tests__/*posting*` finds none). Priority P1 (ACCOUNTING). Basis: code; not executed.
