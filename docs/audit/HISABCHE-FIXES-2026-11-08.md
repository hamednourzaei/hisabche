- Removed: the backend's `OFFLINE_LICENSE_PRIVATE_KEY` / `OFFLINE_LICENSE_KEY_ID` reads, the `LICENCE_KEY_IN_DATABASE` warning, `docs/OWNER-STEP-offline-licence-key-to-env.md`.
