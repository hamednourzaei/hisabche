## What this round does and does not show

- It supports: serial/lot tracking and FIFO/average costing are common at mid-market and up; small-business tools (Xero, Wave, FreshBooks, Pennylane, DATEV, MYOB) are documented as lacking native serial/lot.
- It supports: no cloud-first vendor above documented offline transaction entry in these results (Odoo and ERPNext have limited browser-cached POS; NetSuite mobile is partial). It does **not** prove their absence.
- It does not cover: accounting depth (consolidation, fixed assets, period close), reporting breadth, ecosystem depth, compliance attestations. Those are in round 2.
- Regional products (Sepidar, Rahkaran, Holoo, Hesabfa) returned almost no usable vendor documentation; they cannot be scored from this pass.
