## 2. Invariant & Safety Boundaries

1. **Emphasis only:** Business profile policy NEVER changes ledger posting, FIFO/AVCO cost layers, tax logic, authorization, or role capabilities.
2. **Planned capabilities honesty:** Capabilities with status `planned` in `business-capabilities.ts` are never marked as active or supported.
3. **Tenancy boundary:** `workspace_id` is the security and data boundary. Account-level profiles are strictly fallback for single-workspace legacy owners.
4. **Offline parity:** Both cloud and offline editions share identical canonical schemas, golden vectors, and deterministic policy resolution.
