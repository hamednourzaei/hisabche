## 5. Why `operations.pkg_suspension` stays server-only

Suspending ONE GRANT is a manager's decision recorded as an append-only event with a reason. Offline, two devices could suspend and resume the same GRANT with different intent and the later event would silently win — a financial last-write-wins on entitlement. The matrix row is `online_required`, the UI panel is the only writer, and the count lock (`cloud-required` test) stays at 59 with the reason written there. Reading the status (`GET /package-credits/grants`) works offline on the device.
