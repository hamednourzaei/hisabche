## 1. Camera Language & Spatial Choreography

The camera in a 9:16 vertical reel is not a passive frame; it is an active narrative protagonist that directs merchant attention, creates tension, and delivers clarity.

| Technique                                  | Description & Mechanics                                                                                                                                                                                                               | When to Use for Hisabche                                                                                                                 | Source Reference                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Macro Push-In (Z-Track)**                | Smooth linear optical advance along the Z-axis into microscopic details (ink lines, thermal paper grain, small invoice discrepancy). Focal length shifts or digital scale increases monotonically from `scale(1.0)` to `scale(1.45)`. | Hook moments isolating an alarming discrepancy: a 3,800 Afghani till shortage, an unnoticed tax deduction, or a tiny negative margin.    | [Runway AI Video Guide](https://runway.com/resources/ai-video-prompting-guide) · [HeyDreaming Condensation Macro](https://heydreaming.com/prompts/frost-glass-serum-condensation-macro) |
| **Tracking Shot Parallel (X/Y-Travel)**    | Continuous sideways or vertical tracking that locks onto a moving element while the background slides with parallax motion blur.                                                                                                      | Multi-warehouse goods transit, pallet trucks moving between branch docks, or an invoice moving across 5 accounting stations.             | [Runway AI Video Guide](https://runway.com/resources/ai-video-prompting-guide) · [PromptMotion p-f6775974](https://promptmotion.org/prompts/p-f6775974)                                 |
| **360° Studio Turntable (Orbital Scan)**   | Cylindrical orbit around a central hero object or data card at a steady angular velocity (`12°/s`), catching rim lighting and tactile highlights.                                                                                     | Highlighting precision hardware, gold/silver gram scales, POS barcode scanners, or currency stacks.                                      | [HeyDreaming 360 Turntable](https://heydreaming.com/prompts/sneaker-360-turntable-studio)                                                                                               |
| **Whip-Pan / Whip-Cut**                    | Ultra-fast horizontal camera sweep (`0.12s`) with severe directional blur (`blurX(32px)`) that resolves instantly onto a stark contrasting scene.                                                                                     | Binary split comparisons: from chaotic Excel formula error panic to calm Hisabche ledger balance.                                        | [Runway AI Video Guide](https://runway.com/resources/ai-video-prompting-guide) · [Medeo Video Prompts](https://www.medeo.app/prompts/video)                                             |
| **Low-Angle Heroic Lock**                  | Fixed low-angle perspective looking up at massive typographic headlines or towering warehouse pallet racks (`Y: -15°, scale(1.05)`).                                                                                                  | Establishing structural stability, the cloud engine backbone, or the authority of the double-entry balance sheet.                        | [Runway AI Video Guide](https://runway.com/resources/ai-video-prompting-guide) · [Wade Pixels Guide](https://wadepixels.com/prompts.html)                                               |
| **Continuous Single-Take (One-Shot Flow)** | Unbroken 30-second camera arc traveling from an initial physical anomaly, sweeping through the operational chain, and resolving on the CTA end card without cuts.                                                                     | Proving that a single sales invoice automatically touches stock, drawer, customer credit, ledger, and net profit in one cohesive stroke. | [Oneshotted Showcase](https://oneshotted.io/) · [PromptMotion p-aa15ac75](https://promptmotion.org/prompts/p-aa15ac75)                                                                  |

## 2. Easing Physics & Temporal Dynamics

Physical credibility in motion graphics relies on non-linear mathematical acceleration curves. Banned are robotic linear fades (`ease-in-out` generic defaults) and jarring modulo strobe flickers.

| Easing Model                   | Mathematical Formula / Curve                           | Kinetic Impact & Narrative Meaning                                                                                                                         | Source Reference                                                                                                                                        |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Organic Heavy Deceleration** | `cubic-bezier(0.22, 0.8, 0.2, 1.0)`                    | Elements enter fast from outside the viewport and settle with natural physical friction. Perfect for invoice cards, customer dockets, and ledger panels.   | [HeyDreaming CSS Framework](https://heydreaming.com/prompts) · [Wade Pixels Guide](https://wadepixels.com/prompts.html)                                 |
| **Tactile Studio Spring**      | Mass: 1.0, Stiffness: 180, Damping: 14                 | Crisp mechanical spring bounce that absorbs sudden impacts without oscillation overshoot. Used for rubber-stamp slams, lock badges, and barcode scan hits. | [Production Guardrails](.claude/reel-plugin/production-guardrails.md) · [Oneshotted Remotion Presets](https://oneshotted.io/)                           |
| **Monotonic Odometer Roll**    | `lerp(start, end, smoothStep(t))`                      | Numbers and counters must never flicker, blink, or modulo-wrap backward. Digits roll upwards continuously with constant readability.                       | [Production Guardrails (Rule 1)](.claude/reel-plugin/production-guardrails.md) · [PromptMotion p-539f86ad](https://promptmotion.org/prompts/p-539f86ad) |
| **Infinite Ticker Drift**      | Constant velocity (`18px/s`) with hardware translation | Seamless horizontal ticker scroll of currency exchange rates, gold gram prices, or activity timestamps that maintains secondary micro-motion.              | [Wade Pixels Guide](https://wadepixels.com/prompts.html) · [SoPrompts Directory](https://soprompts.com/free-ai-video-prompts)                           |
| **Climax Impact Micro-Shake**  | `translateX(sin(t * 40) * exp(-t * 6) * 4px)`          | Rapidly decaying 0.25s horizontal vibration following a rejected transaction, a cheque bounce notice, or an audit discrepancy alert.                       | [Production Guardrails](.claude/reel-plugin/production-guardrails.md)                                                                                   |

## 4. Kinetic Persian Typography Choreography

Persian typography requires strict optical calibration: RTL flow, generous line-height to prevent ascender/descender clashes, and word-by-word synchrony with voiceover pacing.

| Typographic Paradigm                 | Animation Spec & Behavior                                                                                                      | Layout & Craft Rules                                                                                                 | Source Reference                                                                                                                             |
| ------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Word-by-Word Karaoke Sync**        | Spoken Persian words trigger active scale pop (`scale(1.15)`) and cyan glow bar overlay (`rgba(8,7,18,0.85)`).                 | Anchored strictly in the caption safe zone (Y: 80%–94%). Words never squeeze the main canvas layout.                 | [Production Guardrails](.claude/reel-plugin/production-guardrails.md) · [WordDrivenStage Engine](hisabche-promo-reel/CLAUDE.md)              |
| **Full-Bleed Hero Headline Rise**    | Words rise vertically through an invisible mask (`translateY(108%) -> translateY(0)`) with `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.7, 0.2, 1.0)`. | Top 10%–25% safe zone. Line-height `1.45–1.75` to preserve Persian glyph ligatures (Shavval, Nastaliq, Dana, Vazir). | [Wade Pixels Hero Typography](https://wadepixels.com/prompts.html) · [PromptMotion p-2c76f525](https://promptmotion.org/prompts/p-2c76f525)  |
| **In-Place Dynamic Verb Flip**       | A key sentence word (e.g. «ضرر» flips mechanically on the X-axis into «سود واقعی») while surrounding context remains locked.   | Arresting the merchant thumb in Frame-0 by shifting the emotional polarity of the statement.                         | [PromptMotion p-48671ce2](https://promptmotion.org/prompts/p-48671ce2) · [CLAUDE Master Constitution](hisabche-promo-reel/CLAUDE.md)         |
| **Optical Character-Length Scaling** | Dynamic font scaling based on Persian string length: `clamp(24px, 6.5vw, 44px)` with `text-wrap: balance`.                     | Eliminates cramped text-in-boxes and awkward single-word wrapping lines. Generous padding (`>= 24px`).               | [Production Guardrails](.claude/reel-plugin/production-guardrails.md) · [HeyDreaming Typographic Hierarchy](https://heydreaming.com/prompts) |

## 7. Sound Design & Acoustic Guardrails

Acoustic engineering is 50% of emotional retention. All audio cues in the prompt library adhere to strict physical authenticity.

| Sound Kind (`SoundKind`) | Acoustic Composition & Spectrum                             | Narrative Role in Video                                       | Prohibited Anti-Patterns                              |
| ------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| `whoosh`                 | 1-pole low-pass filtered air displacement (< 380Hz)         | Camera zooms, slide transitions, perspective whip cuts        | Harsh white noise bursts, synthesized hiss            |
| `impact` / `stamp_slam`  | 55Hz deep sub-bass punch + crisp wood slap transient        | Stamp impacts, deadline alarms, balance lock-ins              | Piercing metal dings, 8-bit arcade clicks             |
| `paper_rustle`           | High-definition organic paper rustle and page turn          | Invoices, thermal receipts, customer dockets                  | Robotic digital sweep sounds                          |
| `printer_feed`           | Mechanical stepper motor hum + thermal receipt feed         | POS print execution, sales receipt ejection                   | Synthetic laser beeps                                 |
| `cash_drawer`            | Mechanical spring slide + heavy steel chime                 | End of shift reconciliation, cash sale settlement             | Cheap coin-drop sound effects                         |
| `clock_tick`             | Muted woodblock escapement tick (`120 BPM`)                 | Countdown tension scenes (cheque clearance, banking deadline) | Synthetic digital watch alarm beeps                   |
| `success_chime`          | Warm harmonic triad chime (E4, G4, B4 ~ 329.6Hz to 493.8Hz) | Reconnection sync, balanced ledger confirmation, CTA reveal   | Piercing microwave beeps, harsh electronic sine pings |
| `silence`                | Deliberate 0.5s–0.8s drop to zero audio right before climax | Maximizing psychological contrast right before the reveal     | Constant wall-to-wall electronic music mud            |
